I am a senior undergraduate student in the **Department of Philosophy at Boğaziçi University** (Istanbul), currently completing an Erasmus+ exchange semester at **Radboud University** (Nijmegen). My work lies at the intersection of **philosophy of technology, AI ethics, and critical theory**, examining how technical systems, computational infrastructures, and historical modes of technicity reshape human agency, political economy, and contemporary culture.

Alongside my academic research, I work extensively in **philosophical publishing, editorial design, and public intellectual commentary**. I serve as an Editor and Layout Designer for *Khôra* (the student philosophy journal of Boğaziçi University) and Assistant Managing Editor for *Çekiçle Felsefe*, where I manage peer review workflows, academic interviews, and print/digital typesetting.

---

### Research Interests
* **Philosophy of Technology & Technics:** Gilbert Simondon, Bernard Stiegler, Yuk Hui, André Leroi-Gourhan, and historical cybernetics.
* **AI Ethics & Political Philosophy:** Human–machine recognition, algorithmic governance, techno-accelerationism, and secular theology.
* **Critical Theory & Aesthetics:** Dialectical materialism, media theory, and representations of artificial intelligence in cinema.
